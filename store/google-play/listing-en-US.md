# KYNIO

## Short description

Track fasting, meals and movement at your own pace.

## Full description

KYNIO is a simple, gamified personal habit tracker.

- Track fasting periods you choose.
- Log meals and review editable calorie and macro estimates.
- Record activities you have already completed, without mandatory plans.
- Earn XP, follow consistency and unlock achievements.
- Keep data locally or connect Google for optional sync.
- Export or delete your data at any time.

Meal analyses are AI-assisted estimates and should always be reviewed manually. KYNIO is a personal
tracking and gamification tool; it does not provide medical, nutritional or training advice.
